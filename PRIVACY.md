# Privacy Policy for Whistle

**Last Updated:** July 2026

At Whistle, we believe your location and personal relationships are strictly your business. Our privacy philosophy is simple: **we do not collect, store, or monetize your personal information.** This policy explains how our app handles your data to keep you connected with the people you choose, without compromising your privacy.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
We do not require you to create an account with us, nor do we ask for your email address, phone number, or real name to operate the core service. 

* **No Developer Servers:** We do not host central databases that store your personal data, contact lists, or location history. 
* **No Analytics or Tracking:** We do not use third-party tracking SDKs to monitor your behavior within the app.

## 2. How Your Shared Data is Handled
Whistle’s core feature is sharing your location with your family or friends. Because of how the app is built, you remain in complete control of this data:

* **Explicit Consent Only:** Your real-time location is only shared with specific groups that you have explicitly joined or invited others to join. 
* **End-to-End Encryption:** When you share your location, the data is cryptographically encrypted on your device (using the Messaging Layer Security protocol). 
* **Photos travel the same way:** If you set a profile avatar, or a group admin sets a group photo, that image is sent *inside* the same end-to-end encrypted messages — seen only by members of your groups, never uploaded to a server, and wiped when you burn your identity.
* **Zero Developer Access:** We (the developers of Whistle) cannot see, intercept, or track your location. Your data flows securely to your trusted contacts, not to us.
* **Diagnostics stay on your device unless you choose to share them:** Advanced Settings can export a diagnostics snapshot to help debug problems. It is generated only when you ask for it, contains no messages, locations, or names — identifiers are truncated — and is never transmitted anywhere automatically. You decide whether, and with whom, to share the file.

## 3. Local On-Device Storage
The data required to make the app work—such as your cryptographic keys, group configurations, and saved nicknames—is stored securely and locally on your own device.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing
Because we do not collect your personal information, we have nothing to sell, rent, or share. We do not partner with ad networks, data brokers, or marketing agencies. 

## 5. Your Control and Permissions
You have ultimate control over your device's data. At any time, you can:

* **Pause Sharing:** Use the in-app controls to instantly stop broadcasting your location to your groups.
* **Revoke Permissions:** Use your iOS Settings to revoke the app’s access to your device’s Location Services.
* **Delete Local Data:** Deleting the app — or using **Burn Identity** in Advanced Settings — permanently wipes your cryptographic keys, messages, and group data *from this device*. Please note this does **not** remove you from groups you have joined: other members keep you on their roster until a group admin removes you, and re-installing with the same key will not restore access. For a clean exit, ask an admin to remove you (or leave the group) before deleting.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
If we ever change the way this app operates (for example, if we introduce a feature that requires us to temporarily process data), we will update this policy and request your explicit permission before any changes take effect.
